DropWheel

Start dragging any file and a wheel pops up beside it. Drop it on a slice to convert it, or flip to Tools to crop, compress, edit, annotate, redact or add a background.

Download for macOS
Free · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple silicon & Intel · All releases

Everything in one gesture

Convert images

JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, TIFF, GIF, BMP and PDF. Pull text out of a picture into TXT or Word with on-device OCR.

Convert PDFs

PDF to Word, JPG, PNG or TXT. Scanned pages are read with OCR. Multi-page PDFs become a folder of images.

Documents

Word, RTF, ODT, HTML and text files become PDF, DOCX, TXT or RTF.

Crop & Edit

Crop with aspect presets, exact pixel sizes, rotate, flip, exposure, contrast, warmth and nine looks.

Add background

Turn screenshots into polished images: gradient, colour or photo backdrops, padding, rounded corners, shadow, social ratios.

Annotate & Redact

Arrows, shapes, pen, highlighter, text. Hide emails, phone numbers and faces automatically.

Compress

Shrink images or PDFs with a live size estimate before saving.

Metadata & privacy

See EXIF data, remove GPS location or strip everything, without re-encoding the photo.

PDF tools

Merge, split, rotate, reorder pages, watermark, password-protect and edit document info.

Remove background

Cut the subject out of any photo in one drop, then give it a new backdrop with Add BG.

Video & audio

MOV ↔ MP4, video to GIF, M4A or WAV. Compress, trim and mute clips.

Batch resize & rename

Resize a stack of images to one size, or rename files with a pattern like Trip-001, with undo.

Install

  1. Download DropWheel.dmg and drag DropWheel into Applications.
  2. The first time, right-click DropWheel and choose Open. It isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks once.
    If macOS still blocks it, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DropWheel.app in Terminal.
  3. DropWheel lives in the menu bar. Drag a file, or press ⌥⌘D.
Everything runs on your Mac. Files are never uploaded.
